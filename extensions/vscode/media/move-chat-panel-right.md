# Move Chat to the right sidebar

### This keeps the Chat panel from covering your file explorer

1. Right-click the **Continuous** icon in the Activity Bar (the left-hand strip of icons).
2. Choose **Move To** → **Secondary Side Bar**.

You can also drag the **Continuous** icon onto the right-hand side of the window.
If the secondary side bar is hidden, open it with **View** → **Appearance** → **Secondary Side Bar**.
